1. Our Privacy Commitment

At KaamSetu, we believe that inviting service professionals into your home requires complete trust. We collect only the minimum necessary information to fulfill your service requests, dispatch verified technicians, and facilitate secure communications. We never sell your personal data or phone number to third-party telemarketers.

2. Information We Collect

Account Credentials Phone number, name, and email address used for OTP-based secure authentication and transaction receipts.
Service Location Address, pincode, and temporary GPS coordinates used solely to route the nearest technician to your residence.
Aadhaar & Police Verification Technicians' government IDs are verified via UIDAI-licensed APIs. Numbers are strictly masked (storing only last 4 digits) with 256-bit AES encryption.
Appliance Passport Records Household appliance models, warranty certificates, and maintenance history stored under your private account control.

3. Security & Telemetry Protection

All communication between your browser and our servers occurs over TLS 1.3 encryption. Real-time GPS location tracking is temporary and automatically terminates as soon as the service booking is marked completed via OTP.
